
Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git keeps track of every change you make to your code, so if you mess something up you can just go back to an older version instead of losing your work.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be used in weather forecasting, like using past weather patterns and sensor data to predict things like storms or flooding before they happen.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging takes two branches and combines them into a new commit, keeping the full history of both. Rebasing instead takes your changes and replays them on top of the other branch, so it looks like a straight line of commits with no extra merge commit.
